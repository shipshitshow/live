## Summary
Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced on March 20, 2026 that AI Studio now integrates the Antigravity coding agent with built-in Firebase support. You describe an app in natural language and Gemini 3.1 Pro builds it — frontend, backend, auth, database — right in your browser. It auto-detects when you need Firestore and Firebase Auth based on your prompt.

## Talking Points
- Prompt-to-production: describe what you want, get a full-stack app with hosting
- Agent proactively sets up Firestore, Auth, and hosting when it detects the need
- Multiplayer support built in — can build collaborative apps from a prompt
- Competing directly with Vercel + Supabase + Cursor workflow
- Firebase integration means instant deployment — no DevOps needed
- Google's play for the "I just want to ship" indie dev crowd

## Hot Take
This is either the biggest threat to Vercel/Supabase or the next Google product that gets killed in 18 months. For indie devs, the allure is real — skip the entire stack setup and just describe your app. But we've been burned by Google's graveyard before. Would you bet your SaaS on this?
