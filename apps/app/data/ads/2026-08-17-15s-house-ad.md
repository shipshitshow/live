# 15s House Ad — Recording Talking Points

Not a script. Four beats, ~35 spoken words total. Say each beat your way,
one breath per beat. Record beats as separate takes so the edit lands on 15s.

## Summary

One 15-second spot that proves the house thesis: everything on this show is a
real product I actually run — genfeed.ai runs the content, mantella.nl runs the
support, shipshit.dev is where you watch it get built. Close with the
non-begging subscribe line.

## Talking Points — Beat 1: The Frame (0–3s, max 7 words)

### Segment Thesis

This isn't sponsor money — it's my own stack, built live on this show.

### Talking Points

- Lead with the conclusion, no wind-up: these are things you built, not things you review.
- Line options:
  - "Everything on this show gets built live."
  - "I don't demo tools. I ship with them."
  - "None of this is sponsored. It's all mine."

### Host Notes

- Don't pretend: no "welcome back", no setup — first word is the claim.
- Energy: statement, not announcement. You're stating a fact, not selling.

## Talking Points — Beat 2: genfeed.ai (3–6.5s, max 9 words)

### Segment Thesis

genfeed.ai is the content operating system — one stream becomes content everywhere.

### Talking Points

- Operator language, not product language: it takes the stream you just watched and turns it into clips, posts, everything.
- Line options:
  - "genfeed.ai — one stream in, content everywhere."
  - "genfeed.ai turns this stream into a week of content."
  - "genfeed.ai runs my content."

### Host Notes

- Say the domain clearly — "genfeed dot A-I". The viewer has one shot to catch it.
- Don't pretend: no feature list. One job, one sentence.

## Talking Points — Beat 3: mantella.nl (6.5–10s, max 9 words)

### Segment Thesis

mantella.nl is support that runs itself — calls come in, tickets come out.

### Talking Points

- This is the "nobody buys the model" beat: the buyer hears "the missed call becomes a ticket", not "AI support platform".
- Line options:
  - "mantella.nl — support tickets that handle themselves."
  - "mantella.nl turns every phone call into a ticket."
  - "mantella.nl runs my support."

### Host Notes

- Say the domain clearly — "mantella dot N-L".
- Don't pretend: don't over-claim automation; "calls in, tickets out" is the honest version we built on stream.

## Talking Points — Beat 4: shipshit.dev + CTA (10–15s, max 12 words)

### Segment Thesis

shipshit.dev is where it all gets built live — subscribe so you don't miss it.

### Talking Points

- Tie the two products back to the show: you watched this get made.
- Line options for the show:
  - "It all ships live at shipshit.dev."
  - "shipshit.dev — watch it get built, every week."
- CTA — house style, never begging, pick one:
  - "Like and subscribe if you're not braindead."
  - "I do this every week. Subscribe so you don't miss it." (brand-safe placements)
- Clip line: **"Like and subscribe if you're not braindead."**

### Host Notes

- CTA lands as the last thing said — nothing after it, hard cut.
- Grin on the braindead line or it reads hostile instead of house-style.
