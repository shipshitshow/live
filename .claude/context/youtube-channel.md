# Ship Shit Show — YouTube Channel Context

## Channels
- **Main:** https://www.youtube.com/@shipshitshow
- **Clips:** https://www.youtube.com/@ShipShitShowClips

## Format
- **Two hosts** — conversational, back-and-forth banter. One leads the topic, the other reacts/challenges.
- **Weekly livestreams** (~1 hour) covering trending AI/dev news
- **Edited videos** (~5-10 min) — tighter cuts from livestream topics or standalone takes
- Streams are simulcast on YouTube + LinkedIn + X

## Niche
AI tools for indie developers. Vibe coding. Developer tools. Solo founder tech stack. OpenClaw, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor.

## Speaking Style (from transcript analysis)
- **Conversational and raw** — not scripted, feels like two devs talking at a bar
- **Frequent code-switching** between serious analysis and joking around
- **Uses "like" and "right?" as filler** — natural spoken cadence, not polished
- **Swears casually** — "[__]" appears naturally, not forced
- **Rhetorical questions** — "Are you still building B2B SaaS in '26? Because if you're not even riding the AI wave, what the [__] are you doing?"
- **Bold declarative openers** — "Software engineering is dead." / "I built my own Zendesk and I did it in three days." / "SaaS is dead."
- **Self-deprecating humor** — "Okay, cool. Nice. Okay, intro done."
- **References real dollar amounts** — "I spent 20 grand on OpenClaw" / "two Claude Max subscriptions which is like 400 a month"
- **Reacts to chat live** — "Yes, you little viewer." / addresses commenters by name
- **Uses ">>" notation** — indicates the second host speaking (co-host dynamic)

## Recurring Phrases & Patterns
- "Let me show you" / "I'm gonna show you"
- "It's insane" / "It's brutal"
- "What the [__] are you doing?"
- "That's kind of cool because..."
- "Here's why"
- "Forget about [X]. Build with [Y]. That's it."
- "We tried. We know."

## Opening Patterns
- **Videos:** Bold claim + immediate context. "Software engineering is dead. If that's your job, you're going to lose it in the next 12 months. Dario from Anthropic said that."
- **Livestreams:** Casual check-in then jump to news. "And yes, let's go. We are live." / "How's your week?" → quick banter → first topic
